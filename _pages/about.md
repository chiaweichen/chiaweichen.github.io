I am a postdoc researcher in the
[Department Visual Inspection Systems (SPR)](https://www.iosb.fraunhofer.de/en/competences/optronics/visual-inspection-systems.html) at 
[Fraunhofer Institute of Optronics, System Technologies and Image Exploitation IOSB ](https://www.iosb.fraunhofer.de) in Karlsruhe, Germany. My research interests are ellipsometry, optical design, precision metrology and scientific machine learning.
